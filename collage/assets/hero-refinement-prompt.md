# Foreground meadow compositing layer

Built-in image_gen. The panorama was a style reference; only a separate transparent foreground layer was generated.

Use case: compositing / transparent photographic collage layer. Input image role: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, the provided wide Russia regional newspaper collage. Do not edit or reproduce that full panorama. Generate ONE NEW SEPARATE FOREGROUND FRAGMENT on a genuinely transparent 2:1 landscape canvas, matching the reference's muted forest-green, ochre meadow and warm cream torn-paper archival halftone photographic texture. Output only a low, gently rolling central grassy meadow hill, with natural dry grass and a few restrained low bushes, fine newsprint grain and a thin irregular pale torn-paper cutout edge. This must look like a photographic fragment cut from old printed paper, NOT a flat vector illustration. Canvas about 1774×887 (2:1). The hill occupies ONLY the lower central part of the canvas. Its highest central crest should start at roughly 62 percent of canvas height (about y=550), and the crest must be smooth and nearly horizontal across about 200 central pixels so small separate rider silhouettes can later stand there in code. From that central crest the hill gently descends toward the bottom on both sides and tapers to nothing before the outermost LEFT 20 percent and RIGHT 20 percent of the canvas, so it will not hide existing architecture or a cottage in the underlying panorama. Keep the whole UPPER 60 percent fully transparent alpha; keep the outer side margins transparent, with no rectangular backing. The lower centre may continue to the bottom canvas edge. Preserve the reference's believable photographic grass textures, subtle forest green/ochre palette, tiny halftone dots, tactile old cream paper edge, quiet editorial tone. No people, hands, riders, horses, buildings, churches, mosques, sun, rays, sky, distant landscape, text, letters, logos or flags. This is a new fictional artistic foreground layer, not a real project photograph. The backdrop will remain a separate untouched image.
